title: Change the next match: cgn
keys: gn  cgn  dgn  n  .
require: cgn :: cgn
require: dgn :: dgn
require: cgn.{1,80}?<Esc>n*\. :: cgn, then dot to repeat it
ext: ts

gn is a text object: "the next match of the last search". With c and dot it
makes a rename you can watch, one match at a time:

  *  or  /text     search first; this sets the pattern
  cgn new Esc      change the match under the cursor, or else the next one
  .                repeat on the following match: no n needed in between
  n                just look at the next match; press n again to leave it
  dgn              delete the next match; dot repeats that too

Unlike :%s you see every match before it changes, and can skip some.
