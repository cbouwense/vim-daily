require: [\d}] ?norm(al)? \S :: :{range}normal
require: g/[^/]{1,40}/norm(al)? \S :: :g/pattern/normal
ext: ts

:g runs an ex command on every line that matches a pattern:

  :g/console.log/d       delete every line that contains console.log
  :v/ERROR/d             the inverse: delete the lines that do NOT match
  :12,20g/TODO/d         a range in front limits it to those lines

:normal types NORMAL-mode keys on each line of a range, as if by hand:

  :12,15normal A;        append ; to lines 12 to 15
  :g/pattern/normal A,   both together: append , to every matching line

With no range, :g and :v scan the whole file and :normal does one line.
