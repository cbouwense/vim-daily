title: Moving with h j k l
keys: h  j  k  l
require: [hjkl] :: h j k l
ext: ts

In NORMAL mode four home-row keys move the cursor:

            k (up)
  h (left)          l (right)
            j (down)

j hangs below the line like a down arrow; h is the leftmost key, l the rightmost.
Your fingers already rest there, which is the point. From today the arrow keys
are switched off in practice. Hold a key down to keep moving. Faster ways to
cross a line come next week. Press Esc to get back to NORMAL mode before moving.
