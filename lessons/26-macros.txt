title: Macros: q @
keys: qa ... q  @a  @@  3@a
require: q[a-z] :: qa
require: @[a-z] :: @a
require: @@ :: @@
require: \d@[a-z] :: a count, as in 3@a
ext: ts

A macro is a recording of your keystrokes that you can play back.

  qa    start recording into register a       q    stop recording
  @a    play the recording                    @@   play the last one again
  3@a   play it three times

Record it so that it works on any line and chains to the next one:
  1. start at a known position: 0, ^ or I
  2. make the change, moving with w, f or $ instead of counting columns
  3. move to the next line with j, then stop with q
One u undoes a whole playback, even 3@a. A bad recording? Just qa again.

== Make each key a call like  await cache.evict("pricing:eu");  On the first
== line: qa, I and the front part, Esc, A and ); Esc, j, q. Then @a, then @@.
"pricing:eu"
"pricing:us"
"catalog:index"
-- qa
-- Iawait cache.evict(<Esc>
-- A);<Esc>
-- jq
-- @a
-- @@

== qa, ^, yiw, A and type up to the first quote, Esc, p, A for the rest, Esc,

const statusLabel = {

== Make each line a call like  tiers.set("gold", 500);  Record: qa, I for the
== front, f and a comma to reach the comma, i for the quote, A for the end,
== j, q. Then 2@a plays it on the other two lines.
gold, 500
silver, 250
platinum, 1000
-- qa
-- Itiers.set("<Esc>
-- f,i"<Esc>
-- A);<Esc>
-- jq
-- 2@a
