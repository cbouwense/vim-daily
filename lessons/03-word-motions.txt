title: Word motions: w b e
keys: w  b  e  (W  B  E)
require: w :: w
require: b :: b
require: e :: e
ext: ts

Moving one letter at a time is slow. Move by word instead:

  w   forward to the start of the next word
  b   back to the start of the previous word
  e   forward to the end of the word

Punctuation counts as a word of its own, so w also stops at "(" and ",".
The capitals W B E only stop at spaces: one W hops over user.profile.name.
Fine-tune with h and l after a hop.

const total = price quantity;

-- i* <Esc>
