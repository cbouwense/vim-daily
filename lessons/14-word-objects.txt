title: Text objects: iw aw
keys: iw  aw  ciw  diw  daw  yiw
require: [cdy]iw :: ciw, diw or yiw
require: [cdy]aw :: daw
ext: java

A text object is a region around the cursor. After an operator it lets you
act on a whole word, wherever in that word the cursor happens to be:

  iw   "inner word": the word itself
  aw   "a word": the word plus the space next to it

  ciw  change the word             yiw  yank the word
  diw  delete the word only        daw  delete the word and its space

cw and dw work from the cursor onward, so you first have to go to the start
of the word. With iw and aw anywhere inside it is fine. Use ciw to replace
a word and daw to remove one without leaving a double space.

== Rename pendingOrders to settledOrders: cursor anywhere in the name, ciw.
private final List<Order> pendingOrders = new ArrayList<>();

-- ciwsettledOrders<Esc>

  counters.clear();
