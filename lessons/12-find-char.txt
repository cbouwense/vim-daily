title: Find a character on the line: f t ; ,
keys: f{char}  t{char}  F{char}  T{char}  ;  ,
require: [dc]\d*t. :: dt or ct
require: [dc]\d*f. :: df or cf
require: (?<![fFtT]); :: ; (repeat the find)
ext: java

f and t jump to a character on the current line:

  f,   forward ONTO the next comma       F,   the same, backward
  t,   forward to just BEFORE it         T,   backward, to just after it
  ;    repeat the last f t F or T        ,    repeat it the other way

After an operator the difference between f and t matters:

  dt)  delete up to the ), keep the )    df)  delete the ) as well
  ct;  change everything before the ;    cf;  change through the ;

Aim at a character that is rare on the line: punctuation, a capital.

}
