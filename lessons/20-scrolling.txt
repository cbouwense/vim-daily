title: Scrolling: Ctrl-d Ctrl-u zz H M L
keys: Ctrl-d  Ctrl-u  Ctrl-f  Ctrl-b  zz  zt  zb  H  M  L
require: <C-d>|<C-f> :: Ctrl-d or Ctrl-f
require: <C-u>|<C-b> :: Ctrl-u or Ctrl-b
require: z[ztb] :: zz (or zt, zb)
require: [HML] :: H, M or L
ext: ts

Move through a long file a screen at a time instead of holding j:

  Ctrl-d   half a page down         Ctrl-u   half a page up
  Ctrl-f   a full page forward      Ctrl-b   a full page back
  H  M  L  cursor to the top (High), Middle or bottom (Low) line you can see
  zz       scroll so the cursor line sits in the centre of the window
  zt  zb   the same, but put it at the top or at the bottom

Scroll until the target is on screen, hop near it with H, M or L, then
finish with j, k or a line number. After a jump that lands at the edge of
the window, zz brings the surrounding code into view.
