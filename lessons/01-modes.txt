require: :wqa?<CR>|:x<CR>|ZZ :: :wq
arrows: yes
ext: ts

Vim has modes. You start in NORMAL mode, where keys are commands, not text.

  i      INSERT mode: what you type is inserted in front of the cursor
  Esc    back to NORMAL mode (when in doubt, press Esc)
  :w     save          :q   quit          :wq  save and quit
  :q!    quit and throw away your changes

Commands that start with ":" appear on the bottom line; Enter runs them.
Today only, the arrow keys move the cursor. Tomorrow you switch to h j k l.
