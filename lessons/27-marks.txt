title: Marks: m ' `
keys: ma  'a  `a  ''  d'a  y'a
require: m[a-z] :: ma
require: '[a-z] :: 'a
require: `[a-z] :: `a
require: '' :: ''
require: d'[a-z] :: d'a
require: y'[a-z] :: y'a
ext: ts

A mark is a named position that you can jump back to.

  ma   set mark a at the cursor (any letter from a to z)
  'a   jump to the line of mark a, on its first non-blank character
  `a   jump to the exact line and column of mark a
  ''   jump back to the line you last jumped from

'a and `a are motions, so operators take them:

  d'a  delete from the cursor line through the line of mark a
  y'a  yank those lines              d`a  delete up to the marked character
